01

What is Vault Breaker?

CONFIRMED BY BUNGIE'S ANNOUNCEMENT // PRESS-CORROBORATED

Vault Breaker drops you into Cryo Archive, Marathon's endgame map, without PvP. You work through a series of progressively harder vaults toward a final vault holding what Bungie describes only as a mysterious entity. Progression is roguelike and unique to the mode: it persists across runs and builds toward that final vault.

The defining rule is that your loot stays behind. Instead of extracting gear, successful runs bank Vault Data, a mode-specific currency you keep and spend on upgrades to Vault Breaker Sponsored Kits or on equipment usable in Marathon's other modes. That keeps the main economy from being flooded from the game's richest zone while still making PvE runs matter.

Bungie has framed the mode as experimental: it is the studio's biggest swing yet at broadening Marathon beyond pure extraction, and it is explicitly designed to be playable solo.

What else ships on July 21?

SAME UPDATE, SMALLER PRINT

The mid-season update also brings the first iteration of the Cradle Evolution system: once your Cradle is fully leveled, you can reset it to zero in exchange for one additional maximum Energy point and unique cosmetics, including Runner shell styles. Cradle progression speed also increases with the update.

Season 3, with its Perimeter revamp, follows on September 22 (countdown above).
